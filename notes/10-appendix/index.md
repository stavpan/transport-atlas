> **How to use the appendix.** It is designed for the moment when you are halfway through a derivation
> and cannot remember whether $K$ is permeability or Kozeny, whether $\mathrm{Da}$ is defined with $L^2/D$
> or $L/U$, or how somebody actually measures a dispersion coefficient in the field. Every entry links
> back to the note that explains the physics.
