# "Convection" is three words wearing one coat

> **Why this note exists.** In heat transfer, fluid mechanics and geophysics, "convection" implies
> different mathematics — and "convective term" means something different again.
>
> **Prerequisites.** [advection](../02-bulk/01-advection.md),
> [convection & buoyancy](../02-bulk/02-convection-and-buoyancy.md)

## The usages

| Usage | Meaning | Mathematics |
|---|---|---|
| **heat-transfer engineer** | combined advection + conduction, often with a correlation: $q=h\Delta T$ | a *modelled* boundary flux, $h$ from $\mathrm{Nu}$ correlations |
| **fluid dynamicist** | buoyancy-driven flow (natural convection); forced or mixed convection by analogy | coupled momentum + energy, $\mathrm{Ra}$, $\mathrm{Gr}$, $\mathrm{Ri}$ |
| **meteorologist** | vertical transport by bulk air motion (including dry convection) | advection with a velocity field, often parameterised |
| **mathematician** | sometimes the whole material derivative, $\mathrm{D}/\mathrm{D}t$ | the transport operator itself |
| **geophysicist** | mantle convection: creeping (infinite-$\mathrm{Pr}$) flow | Stokes + energy equations |

The specific term that causes most trouble: **"the convective term"** in the Navier–Stokes equations,
$\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\mathbf{u}$, is *advection* in the strict sense — the fluid carrying its own
momentum. It is nonlinear self-transport, and calling it "convection" invites confusion with buoyancy.

## The clean distinctions

* **Advection** — passive transport by a velocity field. **Your leaf.**
* **Convection** — transport *with* the motion, where the motion is generated by the transported quantity
  (buoyancy, surface tension) or by an external forcing that makes the transfer "convective" in the
  engineering sense. **The heat making its own wind.**
* **Conduction** — transport with no bulk motion at all
  ([words that collide 3](03-diffusion-and-conduction.md)).
* **Convection vs advection in a model**: if you supply $\mathbf u$ and never solve for it, say
  *advection*; if $\mathbf u$ depends on the transported field, say *convection*.

## The trap

> "Forced convection" is advection with a known velocity field plus conduction. If your code contains a
> prescribed velocity, calling the term "convection" is harmless — but claiming your model *predicts*
> convective enhancement is not, because the velocity came from a correlation, not from the physics.
